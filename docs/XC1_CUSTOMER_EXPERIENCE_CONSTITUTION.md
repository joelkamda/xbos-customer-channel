# XC1 Customer Experience Constitution

Core journey:

`SEE -> CHOOSE -> CONFIRM -> PAY -> KNOW -> RECEIVE`

Laws:
1. No unnecessary registration.
2. No dead-end state.
3. Do not ask twice for known information.
4. Do not expose internal architecture jargon.
5. Every monetary action shows merchant, amount and consequence.
6. Pending states explain what happened and what is safe to do.
7. Authoritative state can resume a journey after interruption.
8. Channel switching preserves the journey.
9. Human handoff preserves context.
10. Mobile/low-bandwidth first.
11. Accessibility/localization are requirements.
12. Corrections preserve understandable history.
13. No surprise fees or silent price changes.
14. Ordinary dine-in requires minimal typing.
15. Every screen/message answers: where am I, what happened, what next?
